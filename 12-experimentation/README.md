# 🧪 12. Experimentation & A/B Testing

> **Evaluating business experiments, calculating sample sizes, and driving evidence-based decisions.**

---

## 📌 Core Steps
1. **Hypothesis Definition**: Control vs Treatment.
2. **Sample Size Calculation**: Statistical Power (80%), Significance Alpha (5%), MDE.
3. **Evaluation**: Two-sample t-tests, Chi-square tests, Confidence Intervals.
4. **Decision**: Evaluating statistical vs practical business significance.
