# Curated Data Analyst Resource Library

> **High-signal, vetted resources for business strategy, SQL, statistics, data visualization, and decision-making.**

---

## 📚 Essential Reading List

### Business & Analytical Thinking
* *Lean Analytics* by Alistair Croll & Benjamin Yoskovitz
* *Measure What Matters* by John Doerr
* *Good Strategy Bad Strategy* by Richard Rumelt
* *Thinking, Fast and Slow* by Daniel Kahneman

### SQL & Data Modeling
* *SQL for Data Analysis* by Cathy Tanimura
* *The Data Warehouse Toolkit* by Ralph Kimball & Margy Ross (Star Schema & Grain)
* *T-SQL Fundamentals / PostgreSQL Developer Guide*

### Statistics & Experimentation
* *Naked Statistics* by Charles Wheelan
* *Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments (A/B Testing)* by Ron Kohavi, Diane Tang, & Ya Xu
* *Practical Statistics for Data Scientists* by Peter Bruce & Andrew Bruce

### Visualization & Storytelling
* *Storytelling with Data* by Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic
* *The Visual Display of Quantitative Information* by Edward Tufte
* *Information Dashboard Design* by Stephen Few
