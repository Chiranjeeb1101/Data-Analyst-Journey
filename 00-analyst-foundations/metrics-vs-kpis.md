# Metrics vs. KPIs & The Metric Hierarchy

> **Distinguishing raw measurements from strategic Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).**

---

## 📊 The Metric Spectrum

```text
RAW DATA POINTS  ──►  METRICS  ──►  KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (KPIs)  ──►  NORTH STAR METRIC
```

| Level | Definition | Example | Business Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Raw Data Point** | Unprocessed transaction or event log | `order_timestamp = 2026-08-12 10:15:00` | Log auditing |
| **Metric** | Any quantitative calculation measuring business activity | Total weekly website visits, Total support tickets submitted | Operational tracking |
| **KPI** | A strategic metric tied directly to a key business outcome | Net Revenue Retention (NRR), 30-Day Customer Retention, CAC payback period | Performance evaluation & target setting |
| **North Star Metric** | The single key metric that best captures long-term product value creation | Paid Weekly Active Accounts with >3 team collaborations | Company-wide alignment |

---

## ⚖️ Leading vs. Lagging Indicators

* **Lagging Indicators**: Measure output after an outcome has occurred (e.g., Quarterly Revenue, Churn Rate). Hard to change quickly.
* **Leading Indicators**: Measure inputs or user behaviors that predict future lagging outcomes (e.g., Weekly Active Feature Usage, Trial Onboarding Completion Rate). Highly actionable!

> 💡 **Analyst Rule**: Track lagging indicators to evaluate performance, but monitor leading indicators to drive proactive intervention!
