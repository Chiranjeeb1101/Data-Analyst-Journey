# Data Analyst Core Glossary & Terminology Index

> **Authoritative reference for business, analytical, statistical, SQL, data modeling, and BI terminology.**

---

## 🅰️ Business & Metrics Terms

* **ARPU (Average Revenue Per User)**: Total revenue divided by the active user base over a specified time period.
* **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)**: Total sales & marketing spend divided by the number of new customers acquired.
* **Churn Rate**: Percentage of customers or subscribers who cancel or fail to renew their service during a given period.
* **LTV (Lifetime Value)**: Total net profit attributed to the entire future relationship with a customer.
* **MRR / ARR (Monthly / Annual Recurring Revenue)**: Predictable recurring revenue earned by a subscription business.
* **North Star Metric**: The single key metric that best captures the core value a product delivers to its customers.
* **Unit Economics**: Direct revenues and costs associated with a single unit of a business model (e.g., per customer, per shipment, per transaction).

---

## 🅱️ Analytical & Statistical Terms

* **A/B Testing**: Controlled experiment comparing two versions (A and B) of a variable to determine which yields better performance.
* **Cohort Analysis**: Analyzing the behavior of a specific subset of users who share a common characteristic (e.g., signup month) over time.
* **Confidence Interval**: A range of values, bounded by statistics, within which an unknown population parameter is estimated to lie.
* **Effect Size**: Quantitative measure of the magnitude of an experimental outcome or statistical relationship.
* **Funnel Analysis**: Tracking user drop-off across sequential steps in a multi-step user journey.
* **p-Value**: The probability of obtaining test results at least as extreme as the observed results, assuming the null hypothesis is true.
* **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary)**: Segmentation model ranking customers based on recency of purchase, frequency of purchase, and monetary value.

---

## 🅲 Data Modeling & Database Terms

* **CTE (Common Table Expression)**: Temporary named result set defined within a execution scope of a SQL statement.
* **Dimension Table**: Table containing descriptive attributes (who, what, where) used to slice and filter numerical measures.
* **Fact Table**: Central table in a star schema containing numerical quantitative measurements (measures) and foreign keys.
* **Grain**: The exact level of detail represented by a single row in a table (e.g., one row per transaction per customer).
* **Star Schema**: Data modeling architecture consisting of one central fact table surrounded by multiple dimension tables.
* **Window Function**: SQL function performing calculations across a set of table rows that are related to the current row without collapsing rows.
