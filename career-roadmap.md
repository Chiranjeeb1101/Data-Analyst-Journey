# Data Analyst Career Roadmap & Progression Framework

> **A strategic, competency-based roadmap mapping growth from foundational analyst to Principal Analyst, Analytics Lead, and Strategic BI Lead.**

---

## 🗺 Progression Stages Overview

```text
STAGE 1: Foundational Analyst
  ├── Execution focused, clean queries, basic dashboards, structured tasks.
  ↓
STAGE 2: Independent Analyst
  ├── Problem-framing, advanced SQL/Pandas, metric design, cross-domain EDA.
  ↓
STAGE 3: Senior / Lead Analyst
  ├── Hypothesis-driven, experimentation, cohort/funnel mastery, executive communication.
  ↓
STAGE 4: Principal Analyst / BI Lead
  ├── Strategic partner, metric tree architecture, business model optimization, org influence.
```

---

## 📊 Detailed Stage Competencies & Standards

### Stage 1: Foundational Analyst (Operational Competency)
* **Business**: Understands basic business models, KPIs (Revenue, CAC, LTV), and common domain terminology.
* **SQL**: Writes SELECT, WHERE, GROUP BY, HAVING, basic JOINs, CASE statements.
* **Excel**: Proficient in VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, SUMIFS, Pivot Tables, basic chart creation.
* **Python/Pandas**: Reads CSV/Excel, basic inspection (`.head()`, `.describe()`), filtering.
* **Visualization**: Builds functional operational reports in Power BI / Excel.
* **Output Standard**: Delivers accurate answers to explicit, structured data requests under guidance.

---

### Stage 2: Independent Analyst (Tactical Mastery)
* **Business**: Maps business functions, identifies unit economic drivers, distinguishes symptoms from root causes.
* **SQL**: Master of CTEs, Window Functions (`ROW_NUMBER`, `LAG`, `LEAD`), Subqueries, aggregation patterns.
* **Excel**: Power Query transformations, dynamic array formulas (`FILTER`, `UNIQUE`), scenario modeling.
* **Python/Pandas**: End-to-end analytical data manipulation, custom aggregation, time-series reshaping.
* **Statistics**: Summary statistics, distributions, hypothesis testing basics, correlation vs causation.
* **Data Modeling**: Understands star schema, grain definition, facts vs dimensions.
* **Output Standard**: Translates semi-ambiguous business questions into clean datasets and quantitative findings.

---

### Stage 3: Senior / Lead Analyst (Strategic Execution)
* **Business**: Partners directly with domain heads (Marketing, Product, Finance, Ops), challenges bad metrics.
* **Advanced Analytics**: Cohort analysis, funnel conversion, RFM segmentation, root-cause decomposition.
* **Experimentation**: Designs A/B tests, determines sample sizes, evaluates statistical & practical significance.
* **Power BI & DAX**: Complex DAX time intelligence, filter context manipulation, executive dashboard design.
* **Data Storytelling**: Writes executive memos answering *What, Why, So What, Now What*.
* **Output Standard**: Converts vague business challenges into structured hypotheses, empirical insights, and clear trade-off recommendations.

---

### Stage 4: Principal Analyst / BI Lead (Strategic & Organizational Influence)
* **Business Strategy**: Evaluates company unit economics, pricing strategies, margin optimization, competitive dynamics.
* **Metrics Architecture**: Designs enterprise-wide North Star metric trees and unified metric dictionaries.
* **Governance & Standards**: Establishes data quality check frameworks, analytical quality checklists, reproducible workflows.
* **Stakeholder Management**: Influences C-suite decisions, defends complex analytical models, navigates organizational ambiguity.
* **Forecasting & Modeling**: Builds time-series trend models and financial/operational scenario models.
* **Output Standard**: Drives measurable revenue/cost business outcomes through data leadership and strategic alignment.
