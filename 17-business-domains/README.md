# 🏢 17. Business Domain Analytics Playbooks

> **Analytics applied across key commercial functions: E-Commerce, SaaS, Logistics, Marketing, Finance, Sales, Product, Ops.**

---

## 📌 Domains Covered
- **E-Commerce**: AOV, Cart Abandonment, LTV, Return Rate.
- **SaaS**: MRR/ARR, NRR, Logo Churn, CAC Payback.
- **Logistics**: On-Time Delivery (OTD), Fulfillment SLA, Lead Time.
- **Marketing**: CAC, ROAS, Campaign Conversion Rate.
