# 📐 06. Applied Business Statistics

> **Statistical rigor for business decision-making — probability, hypothesis testing, and regression.**

---

## 📌 Core Concepts
- **Descriptive Statistics**: Mean, Median, Mode, Variance, Standard Deviation, Percentiles.
- **Probability & Distributions**: Normal distribution, Skewness, Binomial, Poisson.
- **Inferential Statistics**: Sampling, Confidence Intervals, Standard Error.
- **Hypothesis Testing**: p-values, t-tests, Chi-square tests, Statistical vs Practical Significance.
- **Correlation & Regression**: Pearson/Spearman correlation, Linear & Logistic Regression basics.
